# Problem Statement: Groww SIP Discovery Engine

---

## 1. Goal and Business Metric

**Goal:** A public, interactive website that shows why first-time investors on Groww stop or fail to continue a mutual fund SIP. It must let a mentor explore the evidence, test the AI, and see how reliable the AI is.

**Business metric:** % of first-time MF investors who still have an active SIP 12 months after starting.
Active SIP = at least one successful instalment in the last 30 days.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This engine does NOT measure 12-month retention.** Measuring it needs cohort or transaction data. The engine only shows what review writers say breaks SIP continuation.

---

## 2. What the Engine Must Show

The engine must make its entire pipeline transparent. Specifically, it must show:

### 2.1 How data was collected
- A funnel with counts at each step: reviews pulled → mention SIP → relevant → trouble or drop signal.
- Upstream counts (reviews pulled, keyword matches, unique SIP-word reviews) come from `funnel_inputs.json`; everything else is computed from the CSV.

### 2.2 How data was analysed
- Reviews were labelled by an AI (Gemini API) across multiple dimensions: relevance, stage, issue, cause, break type, outcome, hypothesis, income signal, controls mentioned, workarounds, evidence phrase, and insight.
- A `relevant_final` column applies a rule found from the golden set: the AI over-counted praise as a SIP event, so `relevant_final` = No when the issue is Praise or Feature request, even if the AI said `relevant` = Yes.
- The catch-all label "Confused about status or dashboard" was split by a second AI pass into `sub_issue` values.
- `hypothesis_final` and `outcome_final` correct two errors found in the golden check: setup trouble becomes hypothesis E (not A), and a payment failure marked "Paused" becomes outcome "Stalled, not by choice".

### 2.3 How themes were found
- `theme` (not `issue`) is used for every issue chart and table. `theme` = `issue`, except the old catch-all "Confused about status or dashboard" was split into its `sub_issue` values.
- The label "Confused about status or dashboard" must never be shown as a headline theme: only 10 of its 184 reviews were really about confusion.
- Hypotheses A–E are AI-assigned and were not checked on the golden set; they are labelled "directional" on screen.

### 2.4 How insights were produced
- An opportunity table ranks themes by a priority heuristic:
  - **mentions** = relevant reviews in that theme and cause group (denominator = all relevant reviews).
  - **lost rate** = reviews whose outcome is Stopped or cancelled, Switched away, or Stalled not by choice, divided by reviews in the group with a stated outcome (outcome is not Unknown). Set to 0 when fewer than 5 reviews state an outcome.
  - **money flag** = 1 if the theme is about money going wrong (payment failed, wrong debit, money not received), else 0.
  - **priority score** = mentions × (1 + lost rate) × (1 + money flag).
- The score is a prioritisation heuristic, not a measured impact. It tracks mentions closely (rank correlation about 0.94).

### 2.5 How quality was checked
- **Golden set:** AI vs human labels on 30 reviews (`golden_comparison.csv`). Agreement % per field (relevant before and after the rule, stage, issue, cause). Includes a confusion table (human vs AI) for relevance.
- **Second-model audit:** Agreement computed from `audit_result.csv` (AI columns vs `second_model_*` columns, 40 rows). This is a second AI, not a human.
- **Verification level per field:**

| Field | Verification |
|---|---|
| `relevant_final` | Human-checked on 30 golden rows, second-model-checked on 40 rows, one rule added after the golden check |
| `stage`, `issue`, `cause` | Human-checked on only 9 rows, second-model on 13 |
| `theme`, `sub_issue` | Spot-checked by hand on 30 rows, otherwise AI-only |
| `hypothesis_final`, `outcome_final`, `break_type`, `controls_mentioned`, `workarounds`, `income_signal` | AI-only, not verified. Label "directional" wherever shown |

- Pipeline shown in 5 steps: collect, filter, label, check, rank — with counts.

## 4. Data Caveats and Limits

These are copied from the spec:

1. **Play Store only.** Problem reviews skew to one star (67% vs 30% overall).
2. **Mostly English** — about 2% Devanagari, about 10% Hinglish.
3. **Reviews almost never state income** (1 of 721) or whether the user is a first-time investor, so segments come from the survey and interviews.
4. **30 of 1,809 reviews failed to label.**
5. **The SIP-word filter missed some mandate or autopay reviews**, but a sample of those was about IPOs and stocks.
6. **Never claim prevalence.** Play Store problem reviews are 67% one-star versus 30% across all SIP reviews, so counts show patterns, not the share of all users.
7. **`period` splits reviews at 25 Dec 2024**, the date Groww's own letter says it launched "UPI Autopay with Rs 1" and Stocks/ETF SIP. After the date, the share of "Payment failed or missed" fell from about 19% to 9%, so recent data must be easy to see. The "after" group is small.
8. **`scope` marks "Entry (before a SIP is running)"** for setup trouble. It sits before the 12-month cohort and must not be mixed silently with continuation problems.
9. **Feature requests** (issue = Feature request, 319 rows) are excluded from `relevant_final` but must appear in a "Wishes" section on the Themes tab, split by period. Most "stock SIP" requests came before Groww launched stock SIP, so do not call them unmet needs. Weekly or daily MF SIP requests continue after the date.
10. **Support not solving** is shown as a symptom ("amplifier"), not as a root cause.
11. **Hypothesis tags (A to E) are AI-assigned and were not checked on the golden set.** Label them "directional" on screen.
12. **The AI's `confidence` column is NOT reliable** (it said High on every wrong answer in testing). Do not use it as the quality gate.

## 5. Rules

1. **No investment advice anywhere.** Footer on every screen: "Research tool. Not investment advice."
2. **No personal data.** Show only the project's own `evidence_id`. Never show any Play Store ID or reviewer name.
3. **`evidence_id` only.** The AI may cite only `evidence_id` values that exist in the data (max 5 per claim).
4. **No hardcoded numbers.** Every number comes from the CSV files. Upstream counts come from `funnel_inputs.json`, everything else from the CSV. No hardcoded counts in code.
5. **Fallback when the API fails.** Every AI call has a rate limit (10 per visitor session) and a fallback. If the quota is hit or the API fails, show saved example results and say so. Never show a raw error.
6. **Show `n` on every chart.** Mark any group with n < 30 as "small sample, pattern only".
7. **Colour-blind-safe palette.** Minimum font 14. Works on a phone.
8. **Load time under 3 seconds** after first start.
9. **API key lives in server secrets** (`st.secrets`), never in code or the page.

## 6. Open Questions

1. **`funnel_inputs.json` references `labelled_reviews_final.csv`** in its `note` field, but the actual data file in the folder is named `labelled_reviews.csv`. Are these the same file, or is there a separate final version?
2. **The spec says "relevant_final = Yes should give 721 rows, 513 before and 208 after"** — these are stated as validation-only reference values. Should the engine surface these as test assertions in code, or are they purely for manual verification?
3. **"Where the AI was wrong": 2–3 examples with the fix** — the spec says content will be added later in `evidence_notes.md`. This file does not exist yet. What should the engine show in the meantime?
4. **Research tab (Tab 9) is marked optional** — "Build last, only if time is left. Skip if not." Should we plan for it at all, or exclude it from scope entirely?
5. **The golden comparison file uses IDs G01–G30** while the main data uses E0001–E1809. Some golden rows map to evidence_ids in `audit_result.csv` (e.g., G17's text matches E0106). Is there a mapping file, or should we match by review text?
6. **Hypothesis E ("setup or autopay blocked") was added as a correction** for setup trouble (issue = "Cannot start or set up SIP"), which the AI wrongly called hypothesis A. Is this correction already applied in `hypothesis_final` in the CSV, or must the engine apply it at runtime?
7. **The spec mentions "controls_mentioned (pipe separated)" and "workarounds (pipe separated)"** as columns in the data description, but these columns are not listed among the Evidence browser filters. Are they intentionally excluded from filtering, or should they be included?
8. **Period filter scope:** The spec says to add a period filter (All / Before / After) on Overview, Themes, Opportunities, and Evidence. Should the Journey heatmap (Tab 3) also support the period filter?
9. **Suggested questions for "Ask the data" (Tab 6):** The spec says 6 suggested questions but does not list them. Should the builder define them, or will they be provided?
10. **The `note` in `funnel_inputs.json` says "Later steps must be computed from labelled_reviews_final.csv."** — does "later steps" mean all counts below `sip_word_unique` in the funnel (i.e., relevant count, trouble/drop signal count) should be computed from the CSV at runtime?
